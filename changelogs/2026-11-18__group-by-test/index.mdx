The build sidebar listed snapshots by status: every change of the build in one section, whatever test it came from. The new **Display options** button, next to the filter button, lists them **by test** instead, and as **names** when screenshots take too much room.

- **One section per test**: named by the test's title and file, with its snapshots in the order the test took them. A test run by several Playwright projects is one section, and Storybook snapshots group by component.
- **Most pressing first**: tests with a failure or a change come first and start open. Tests where nothing changed wait, collapsed, at the bottom.
- **A status on every snapshot**: each one shows its status icon, accepted or rejected included, and each test shows its most pressing one.
- **Names only**: a compact list of names, to scan a build of hundreds of snapshots at a glance.

Both choices are kept in your browser, and switching keeps the snapshot you are reviewing in view.

Learn more in [Review a build](https://argos-ci.com/docs/learn/review-workflow/review-a-build#choose-how-the-snapshot-list-is-shown).
