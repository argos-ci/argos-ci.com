SAML SSO added people to your team on their first sign-in, and someone had to remember to remove them when they left. **SCIM provisioning** hands the team to your identity provider: assign someone to Argos and they are a member, deactivate them and they lose access.

- **Members before their first sign-in**: Okta, Microsoft Entra ID or any SCIM 2.0 identity provider creates them, linked to their Argos account through a verified email, or to a new one.
- **Offboarding in one place**: unassigning or deactivating someone removes them from the team on the spot. Their Argos account and their other teams stay theirs.
- **Roles from your directory**: map `owner`, `member` and `contributor` through the standard `roles` attribute, for example with Entra ID app roles.
- **Safe by default**: the last owner is never demoted, and the settings warn you when your team has a single owner.

Set it up from **Team Settings -> Authentication**: generate a token, then give your identity provider the token and the base URL.

```
https://api.argos-ci.com/scim/v2
```

SCIM provisioning comes with SAML SSO, in the Enterprise plan and in the SAML SSO add-on.

👉 [Set it up with the SCIM provisioning documentation](https://argos-ci.com/docs/learn/account-and-access/scim-provisioning)
