Add a banner to a page and everything below it moves. Compared pixel by pixel, the whole page turned red, and the one real change got lost in it. Argos now detects **layout shifts** and shows them apart from what changed.

- **Only the real changes in red**: the overlay leaves out content that only moved, and so do the change highlights, the previous and next change navigation and the markers beside the screenshot.
- **Moved blocks, outlined**: each block that moved is outlined in both panes, where it was and where it is now, with the direction and distance it moved, such as ↓ 48 px.
- **One key to hide them**: toggle the outlines with the **Layout shift** button in the toolbar, or `Shift`+`D`.
- **Nothing else changes**: scores, fingerprints and ignore rules work as before, and a snapshot whose content only moved still needs review.

## Exact, never guessed

Argos compares screenshots with [iris](https://github.com/argos-ci/iris), its open-source diff engine. While it compares two screenshots, iris looks for blocks of rows that appear at another height. A block counts as moved only when its pixels are identical in both, so a move is never invented. iris detects vertical moves, and content that moved and changed is reported as a change. Detection applies to new comparisons; earlier builds keep their full diff.

Learn more in the [layout shifts documentation](https://argos-ci.com/docs/learn/platform-fundamentals/how-argos-detects-visual-differences#layout-shifts).
